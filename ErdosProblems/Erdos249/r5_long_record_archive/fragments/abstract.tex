\begin{abstract}
For every $e\ge1$, the dyadic sections of Euler's totient through level $e$
have rational rank $2^e+1$. The two zero-residue sections and the odd-residue
sections form a basis, and two families of scalar reductions generate every relation.
A diagonal evaluation matrix proves independence; the same construction gives
rank $k^e+1$ in every integer base $k\ge2$ and an integral normal form.
Separately, $\sum_{n\ge1}(\ph(n)\bmod m)2^{-n}$ is irrational for every
$m\ge3$, with a complete rationality classification at dyadic moduli.
For the unreduced series $S=\sum_{n\ge1}\ph(n)2^{-n}$, one nonintegral tail
shift produces full-depth certificates on an eventually two-syndetic set.
The remaining equivalent obligation is an explicit central-residue gap for
every prospective denominator. It is not proved here.
\end{abstract}
